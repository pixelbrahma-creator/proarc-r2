One practice, accountable from first approval to handover

We bring architecture, structure and building services together in one office, so the scheme that is approved is the scheme that gets built — and we stay with it through construction.

Schools, malls, offices and homes — one practice answers for all of them.

The smallest building here and the tallest carry the same responsibility.

Every project starts with the site, the budget and the approvals it will need. Then we draw.

Master planning

Site-wide strategies and urban masterplans at Ajman Hills, Al Zahya Gardens, Global City and Zahya Hills — the framework every building on the site then has to work within.

Architecture

The brief, the site, the approvals it will need, and the drawings that answer them — concept through to construction issue.

Structural engineering

The frame, drawn in-house alongside the architecture rather than issued to it.

MEP engineering

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing, coordinated from concept stage, so the services fit the building they were drawn for.

Interior design

Spatial planning, materials and lighting, detailed to the same drawings as the envelope.

Project management

Programme, cost and contract administration from appointment to handover, with our own resident engineers on site through construction.

Our specialist experience includes:

  • Authority submissions and approvals
  • Feasibility studies
  • Concept and scheme design
  • Construction drawings
  • Structural design and detailing
  • MEP coordination and clash detection
  • Services load calculations
  • Site supervision and quality inspection
  • Snagging and handover
  • Space planning
  • Material and finish selection
  • Lighting design
