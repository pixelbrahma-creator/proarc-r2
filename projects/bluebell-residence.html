Bluebell Residence

A landmark high-rise residential development in Al Aamra, Ajman, combining modern urban living with architectural elegance.

Bluebell Residence — project facts
ClientGJ Properties
LocationAamra, Ajman
Configuration2B+G+6P+42F

Set in the rapidly developing community of Al Aamra, Ajman, Bluebell Residence is a contemporary high-rise residential development designed to provide modern urban living with exceptional comfort, functionality, and architectural elegance. Developed for GJ Properties, the project reflects a commitment to quality construction, efficient planning, and an enhanced residential lifestyle.

The development features a striking modern façade with a balanced composition of glass, textured cladding, and warm neutral finishes that create a distinctive identity within Ajman's growing skyline. The podium incorporates an elegant architectural screen, providing both aesthetic appeal and practical shading while accommodating ample parking facilities.

Currently under construction, Bluebell Residence has been thoughtfully planned to meet the demands of today's residents by combining spacious living environments with premium amenities. The project consists of a Ground Floor, six podium parking levels, twenty typical residential floors, a dedicated health club floor, and a roof level, offering residents a complete and convenient lifestyle within a single integrated development.

With a total built-up area of 80,897.97 square meters, the project is designed to maximize space efficiency while ensuring natural lighting, ventilation, and panoramic views of the surrounding cityscape.
