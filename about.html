We are an architecture, engineering and project management practice in Ajman.

One office, one team, working across the Emirates since 2006.

A city is not a set of sectors. It is where people learn, shop, pray, work and live — and here we have drawn all of them. The schools our clients’ children attend, the malls their neighbourhoods use, the mosque they pray at, the offices they work in and the homes they return to were, often enough, the work of one team.
